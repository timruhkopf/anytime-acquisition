## §2 Pilot (2026-11-11)

**What ran.** 4000 states (826 test states), d = 2 (`results/api_probe/pilot_20261111-163954`, code 5ce73d2). PFN passes took 10.5 min on a GPU shared with two other runs. Analysis: notebook 08.

**Result.**
- Readable from the PFN's state, and from all layers better than from the last (paired CIs > 0, ridge):
  - x\*: R² 0.527 all vs 0.445 last vs 0.000 hand;
  - number of relevant inputs: Spearman 0.814 vs 0.801 vs 0.538;
  - roughness: 0.929 vs 0.910 vs 0.870.
- Not readable:
  - the advantage label: within-state Spearman ≤ 0.025 for every input, although the labels' split-half reliability is 0.568;
  - headroom_bin: AUC 0.681 from hand features, 0.616 from all layers (all − hand = −0.065 [−0.116, −0.011]).
- KG is readable: within-state Spearman 0.31–0.33 from hand features, 0.26–0.29 from all layers.

**Interpretation.** The 0006 all-layer readout is supported for describing the state: where the optimum is, and the landscape. Neither the deviation decision nor the ranking of candidates by their clairvoyant advantage is linearly or shallowly readable, from PFN states or from hand features. Whether a purpose-trained decoder composes them is the question of notebook 09.

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE** (pre-registered rule; recorded with `decide`).

**Next.** Notebook 09 (decoder pilot, running). An open question for later: does KG at the labeled candidates predict their labels (a direct check, not yet run)?

## §3 Addendum: direct scores against the label (2026-11-11)

**What ran.**
- The pilot configuration again, plus a `direct` table (`results/api_probe/pilot_20261111-204422`, code fc305ea).
- This is post hoc and exploratory, recorded in notebook 08 before the run. It was prompted by the gate pilot's Spearman of 0.042 (api_gate §2).

**Result.**
- Every probe value of §2 is reproduced exactly.
- Within-state Spearman of untrained scores against the advantage label, at all 2992 labeled states:
  - log EI 0.063 [0.051, 0.075]; PI 0.063;
  - KG 0.048 [0.037, 0.060];
  - rel_q05 −0.075, rel_mu −0.062;
  - sd −0.022.
- The label reliability is 0.568.

**Interpretation.**
- No belief quantity ranks the candidates. This holds for trained readouts (§2), the PFN lookahead (api_gate §2) and KG.
- The clairvoyant headroom over EI at these states is not information the PFN's belief contains. It is outcome luck or PFN error, which a policy conditioned on the belief cannot exploit.

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE.** Unchanged: the pre-registered rule on the reproduced tables, recorded with `decide` for this sweep.
