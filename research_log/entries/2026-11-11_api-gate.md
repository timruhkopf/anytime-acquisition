## §2 Pilot (2026-11-11)

**What ran.**
- 2000 states and 200 split-1 policy tasks, d = 2 (`results/api_gate/pilot_20261111-164326`, code 5ce73d2).
- Timing on a GPU shared with two other runs: states 76.5 min, policy 160.7 min.
- Analysis: notebook 07.

**Result.**
- Stage 1b: mb − EI on norm_regret is +0.028 [−0.58, 0.74], relative +1.9% [−38%, +49%].
  - The policy deviates at 86% of steps.
  - It is better on 39.5% of tasks and tied on 18%.
- Stage 1a at τ = 0:
  - G = 0.080 [−0.025, 0.19] % of the gap;
  - a random valid deviation gives 0.035 [−0.019, 0.088];
  - the in-sample best of the 16 candidates gives 0.89 [0.71, 1.10], which is upward-biased.
- Within-state Spearman between the model advantage and the label: 0.042 [0.026, 0.057] over 1526 states.
- G by step: about 0.2 for t < 10 and about 0 for t ≥ 10. These numbers are from `tables/gate_states.parquet`.

**Interpretation.**
- Belief continuations from the PFN barely rank candidates by their true advantage. This agrees with the probes (notebook 08).
- The clairvoyant headroom of notebook 06 is mostly not plannable from the PFN's own belief at this budget (16 candidates, 4 continuations).
- Stage 1b is too noisy at 200 tasks to decide, but Stage 1a gives no reason to scale it.
- Fine-tuning the PFN on BO contexts (0004/0005 E1) looks more promising than amortizing a belief-only lookahead.

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE** (pre-registered rule; recorded with `decide`).

**Next.** Do not scale Stage 1b. Wait for the decoder pilot (notebook 09), which tests learning from ground-truth labels instead.
