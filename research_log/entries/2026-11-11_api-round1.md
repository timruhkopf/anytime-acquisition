  because c\* is chosen only within the grid.

Tim's baseline decision was relayed by the bbob-hedge session and recorded on `exp/bbob-hedge` (57ae2d9, log entry
2026-11-10_bbob-hedge §4). ZeroShotOpt [meindl2025zeroshotoptzeroshotpretrainedmodels], arXiv:2510.03051, is an

The api_round1 pilot was pre-registered before this decision and runs on BNN-prior tasks, so it is unchanged. Later
api experiments (the 0006 decoder, and any BBOB transfer of the learned policy) should list ZeroShotOpt in their
baselines, with the same protocol as bbob-hedge §4:
- the same split-1 tasks, budget and initial design;
- the paired gain with a bootstrap CI, next to GP-Hedge, the ceiling and the null.

Check before running it:
- how regret-to-go is set at test time (never tuned on split 1);
- its 12 behaviour variants and its normalization;
- whether released weights cover our d;
- its licence.
On BNN-prior tasks it is out of its training distribution (it was pretrained on GP-prior draws). That makes it a
transfer baseline there, and it must be reported as such.
