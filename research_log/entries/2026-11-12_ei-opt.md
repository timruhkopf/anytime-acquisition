See the description at the top of `notebooks/11_2026-11-12_ei_opt.ipynb`; not retyped here.

**What ran.** 100 states and 50 rollout tasks × 4 replicates × 4 variants on ulysses (`results/ei_opt/sanity_20261112-090427`, code 295a9b9). It took 5.3 min: states 2.4, rollouts 2.9.

**Result** (a pipeline check; 50 tasks is far too few for the decision):
- Part A, pick distance between replicates (mean): sampled 0.053; sampled+refine 0.029; fixed and fixed+refine 0 by construction.
  - In 87% of states the sampled replicate picks differ by more than 1e-3; after refinement, in 11%.
  - The remaining spread of sampled+refine sits at gap < 1 bin (0.036) and > 100 bins (0.074): EI has several near-equal maxima there.
- Part A, EI ratio (median / q10): sampled 0.993 / 0.939; fixed 0.995 / 0.939; both refined variants 1.000.
- Part B, BO on split 0 (paired, relative, norm_regret):
  - fixed+refine − sampled: +7.6% [−24%, +48%];
  - fixed − sampled: −17% [−39%, +3%].
  - q95 of per-task regret: refined variants 4.9, sampled 2.6, fixed 2.2.
  - Replicate determinism: the fixed variants are deterministic on 100% of tasks; sampled on 24%.
- Verdict on the sanity run: INCONCLUSIVE, as expected at this size.

## §2 Pilot (2026-11-12)

**What ran.**
- 2000 labeled states (Part A) and 1000 split-0 tasks × 4 replicates × 4 variants (Part B), d = 2 (`results/ei_opt/pilot_20261112-091043`, code 295a9b9).
- It took 26 min on ulysses: states 3.4 min, rollouts 22.8 min.
- Analysis: notebook 11.

**Result.**
- Sampled EI is stochastic but near-optimal in value:
  - replicate picks differ by more than 1e-3 in 90% of states;
  - EI ratio median 0.995, q10 0.944;
  - replicate sd of the final normalized regret: median 0.0072, mean 0.79.
- Refinement determinism: `fixed+refine` is deterministic on 100% of tasks. `sampled+refine` still differs by more than 1e-3 in 11% of states, mostly within one bin of f\*.
- Normalized regret, `fixed+refine` − `sampled`: −5.7% [−19%, +7%].
- Log regret, paired Δ in log10 units (positive = worse):
  - `fixed+refine` +0.056 [0.013, 0.100];
  - `sampled+refine` +0.058 [0.018, 0.098];
  - `fixed` −0.029 [−0.074, 0.015].
- Tail: q95 of the normalized regret 7.9–8.7 and failure rate 0.079–0.083, about equal across variants.

**Interpretation.**
- Refining EI does not help. On log regret, the scale that rewards progress near the optimum, it is significantly worse, by about 14% in geometric-mean regret.
- The unrefined `fixed` candidate set gives a deterministic π₀ that is indistinguishable from the current EI on both scales. That reading is post hoc: the rule did not cover `fixed`.

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE** (pre-registered rule on `fixed+refine`; recorded with `decide`).

**Next.** A deterministic π₀ for relabeling: `fixed` (unrefined), confirmed under the reward that will be used (log regret, anytime), as part of the relabeling design.
