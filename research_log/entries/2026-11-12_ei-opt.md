## §1 Sanity (2026-11-12)

**What ran.** 100 states and 50 rollout tasks × 4 replicates × 4 variants on ulysses (`results/ei_opt/sanity_20261112-090427`, code 295a9b9). It took 5.3 min: states 2.4, rollouts 2.9.

**Result** (a pipeline check; 50 tasks is far too few for the decision):
- Part A, pick distance between replicates (mean): sampled 0.053; sampled+refine 0.029; fixed and fixed+refine 0 by construction.
  - In 87% of states the sampled replicate picks differ by more than 1e-3; after refinement, in 11%.
  - The remaining spread of sampled+refine sits at gap < 1 bin (0.036) and > 100 bins (0.074): EI has several near-equal maxima there.
- Part A, EI ratio (median / q10): sampled 0.993 / 0.939; fixed 0.995 / 0.939; both refined variants 1.000.
- Part B, BO on split 0 (paired, relative, norm_regret):
  - fixed+refine − sampled: +7.6% [−24%, +48%];
  - fixed − sampled: −17% [−39%, +3%].
  - q95 of per-task regret: refined variants 4.9, sampled 2.6, fixed 2.2.
  - Replicate determinism: the fixed variants are deterministic on 100% of tasks; sampled on 24%.
- Verdict on the sanity run: INCONCLUSIVE, as expected at this size.
